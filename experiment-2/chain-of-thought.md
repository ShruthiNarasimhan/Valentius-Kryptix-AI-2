# Experiment 2B: Step-by-Step Prompting

## 1. Objective

To evaluate how explicitly requesting step-by-step reasoning influences the structure and accuracy of an LLM's response when solving a multi-step mathematical problem.

## 2. Experimental Setup

- Model: Google Gemini (verify exact model version)
- Platform: Google AI Studio
- Task: Profit and Loss Calculation
- Prompting Technique: Step-by-Step Prompting

## 3. Prompt Used

You are a mathematics problem-solving assistant.

Solve the following Profit and Loss problem by explaining each calculation clearly and in the correct order.

A shopkeeper purchases 40 notebooks at ₹25 each. He sells 30 notebooks at ₹35 each and the remaining 10 notebooks at ₹20 each.

Calculate the following:

1. Total Cost Price.
2. Selling Price of the first 30 notebooks.
3. Selling Price of the remaining 10 notebooks.
4. Total Selling Price.
5. Profit or Loss.
6. Profit or Loss Percentage.

Show the formula used for each calculation, substitute the given values, and explain the result briefly.

Present the final answers in a clear summary.

## 4. Actual Model Output

**Paste the complete, unedited response generated by Gemini here.**

Include all calculation steps and the final summary exactly as returned by the model.

## 5. Expected Results and Evaluation

| Calculation | Expected Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Cost Price | ₹1,000 |
| Selling Price of first 30 notebooks | ₹1,050 |
| Selling Price of remaining 10 notebooks | ₹200 |
| Total Selling Price | ₹1,250 |
| Profit | ₹250 |
| Profit Percentage | 25% |

**Evaluation:** Compare the model's response against all six expected results.

## 6. Observation

The prompt explicitly requested formulas, substitutions, explanations and a final summary.

The recorded response produced the correct mathematical results and presented the calculations in an organized manner.

Compared with direct prompting, step-by-step prompting made the expected response structure more explicit. However, both techniques achieved the same mathematical accuracy in this experiment.

## 7. Conclusion

Step-by-step prompting successfully solved the given mathematical problem.

The experiment demonstrated that explicit instructions can encourage a more structured explanation, although they do not necessarily improve the final answer's accuracy.
