# Experiment 2A: Direct Prompting

## 1. Objective

To evaluate how Google Gemini solves a
multi-step mathematical problem using
a direct prompt.

## 2. Experimental Setup

- Model: Gemini 3.8 Flash
- Platform: Google AI Studio
- Technique: Direct Prompting
- Task: Profit and Loss Calculation

## 3. Prompt Used

Solve the following mathematical problem
and provide the final profit or loss and
profit/loss percentage.

A shopkeeper buys 40 notebooks at ₹25 each.
He sells 30 notebooks at ₹35 each and the
remaining notebooks at ₹20 each.

Calculate:
1. Total cost price.
2. Total selling price.
3. Total profit or loss.
4. Profit or loss percentage.

## 6. Observation

Although the prompt directly requested
the final profit or loss and percentage,
Gemini independently generated a detailed
step-by-step explanation.

The model correctly calculated the total
cost price, selling price, profit and
profit percentage.

This demonstrates that a direct prompt
can also produce detailed explanations
depending on the model's response style.
