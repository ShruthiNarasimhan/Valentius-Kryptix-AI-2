# Experiment 2A: Direct Prompting

## 1. Objective

To evaluate the ability of an LLM to solve a multi-step mathematical problem using a direct prompt without explicitly requesting step-by-step reasoning.

## 2. Experimental Setup

- Model: Google Gemini (verify exact model version)
- Platform: Google AI Studio
- Task: Profit and Loss Calculation
- Prompting Technique: Direct Prompting

## 3. Prompt Used

A shopkeeper purchases 40 notebooks at ₹25 each. He sells 30 notebooks at ₹35 each and the remaining 10 notebooks at ₹20 each.

Calculate:

1. Total Cost Price.
2. Selling Price of the first 30 notebooks.
3. Selling Price of the remaining 10 notebooks.
4. Total Selling Price.
5. Profit or Loss.
6. Profit or Loss Percentage.

Provide the final answers clearly.

| Calculation | Expected Result |
|---|---:|

| Selling Price of remaining 10 notebooks | ₹200 |
| Total Selling Price | ₹1,250 |
| Profit | ₹250 |
| Profit Percentage | 25% |

**Evaluation:** The model's response should be compared against all six expected results.

## 6. Observation

The direct prompt asks Gemini to solve the problem and provide the final answers clearly without explicitly specifying a step-by-step response format.

The recorded response produced the correct mathematical results. Gemini also included calculation details even though the prompt did not explicitly request a detailed explanation.

## 7. Conclusion

Direct prompting successfully solved the given Profit and Loss problem.

The experiment demonstrates that a direct instruction can be sufficient for obtaining correct results in a multi-step mathematical task.
